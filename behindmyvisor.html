Behind my Visor!

I'm Hrishikesh H L (Harry).
Mechanical engineering student by degree, race junkie by choice, and currently trying to convince my laptop to not crash mid-simulation at 3AM.

This site? It's not a portfolio. It's a pitstop for all the chaos in my head — lattice defects, chai-fueled rants, and a dream that smells like burnt clutch and scorched carbon-carbon brake discs. If you're here for clean, curated engineering, Bud, wrong garage.

I didn't grow up building cars or fabricating composites — I just watched one too many "how to machine a knuckle" & "Rust to Riches" episodes and now I think I'm hooked to the science behind the materials around us. I haven't set anything on fire yet, but I'm emotionally ready. I crave the late nights, the existential crisis over a misaligned spline which gives a zero dimension error. Right now I mostly lurk, learn, and pretend to understand nano-composite layups — but give it time. I'm already in too deep, and I haven't even ruined my first tap set. I'm neck-deep in Python scripts, FEA errors on carbon-nanotube reinforced matrices, and that one material library file that won't load. But the more I learn, the deeper I spiral into this beautiful, atomic mess of motorsport evolution.

This isn't just about engineering. Or racing.
It's about me — my breakdowns, breakthroughs, molecular level mayhem, poetry, playlists and confessions I've chosen to make public. Sometimes it's code. Sometimes it's chaos. Sometimes it's both.

Why "Desi Downshift"?
Because every time I hit the brakes, heel-toe like a maniac, and shift down, I remember where I started—from desi streets of Coimbatore to racing dreams. This isn't just engineering. It's attitude, grit, and desi-engineered chaos control.

So, welcome to Desi Downshift — torque down your doubts, and take the ride!
